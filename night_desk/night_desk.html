    Read-only. Data comes from Hyperliquid's public info API, directly or through night_desk_server.py; nothing here can place or cancel an order. Accounts refresh every 15 s, fills and equity every 30 s, market and chart every 60 s, paused while the tab is hidden. "24h" figures are Hyperliquid's rolling 24-hour window. Funding is charged hourly; annualised figures are the hourly rate × 8,760. Strategy evidence is excess Sharpe over random entry, measured on data the strategy was never fitted to.
