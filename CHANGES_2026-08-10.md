### 1. Stop 0.24 → 0.40  ✅ APPLIED

`bot/config.py:155`. Also synced `AGGREGATE_STOP_FRAC_DEFAULT` (portfolio.py) and
two stale comments.

The 0.24 setting was chosen because tightening appeared to raise Sharpe. Two
measurement errors inflated that:

- **`reference_impl.run_sleeve` ERASED the loss on the trigger day** — it zeroed
  the weight *before* applying that day's return, so the day that fired the stop
  booked exactly £0 instead of the loss that caused it. Measured inflation:
  **+0.92 Sharpe at 0.24**, +0.28 at 0.60. Tighter stop → fires more → erases more.
- Even fixed, the model books the loss **at** the stop level — a perfect fill.
  The project's own intrabar test measured **3.75% median extra slippage** and
  **42% of stops firing a full day early**.

With both priced in:

| stop | fires/yr | modelled | with slippage |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.60 | 1.7 | 1.75 | 1.73 |
| **0.40** | **3.9** | 1.88 | **1.82** |
| 0.24 | 18.7 | 2.16 | 1.90 |
| 0.20 | 30.9 | 2.30 | **1.88** ← goes backwards |

**Time-to-pass is identical at 0.40 and 0.24** (E[29] vs E[28] @ $4k/day), so
0.40 costs nothing and fires **5× less often** — far less dependence on fills
behaving as modelled. The sweep is monotonic to **4.93 Sharpe at a 5% stop**,
which proves the "gain" is a modelling artifact, not edge.
